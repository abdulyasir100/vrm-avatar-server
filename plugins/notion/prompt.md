### notion_append / notion_create
- [TOOL:notion_append:<page id>|Bought the tickets, 21 Sept.] adds a paragraph to the end of the page.
- [TOOL:notion_create:<parent page id>|Packing list|- passport\n- charger] creates a page under the parent.
- Only pages the user shared with the integration exist for you. A "not found" means it is not shared yet.
